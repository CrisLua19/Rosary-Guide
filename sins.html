⚔ Seven Deadly Sins

Overcome sin through virtue • "Resist the devil and he will flee from you" - James 4:7

The Seven Deadly Sins are the root of all sin. Each has a corresponding virtue to cultivate in our hearts. Pray daily to overcome these vices and grow in holiness.

👑

Pride

Excessive love of self

Opposite Virtue: Humility
"Lord, help me see myself as You see me."
💰

Greed

Excessive desire for wealth

Opposite Virtue: Charity
"All I have is Yours, Lord."
❤️‍🔥

Lust

Disordered desire for sexual pleasure

Opposite Virtue: Chastity
"Purify my heart, O Lord."
😠

Envy

Resentment of others' success

Opposite Virtue: Kindness
"Rejoice in others' blessings."
🍽️

Gluttony

Overindulgence in food/drink

Opposite Virtue: Temperance
"My body is Your temple."
🔥

Wrath

Uncontrolled anger

Opposite Virtue: Patience
"Grant me peace, Lord."
😴

Sloth

Laziness toward good works

Opposite Virtue: Diligence
"Give me zeal for Your kingdom."

Daily Examen Prayer

1. Give thanks for God's blessings today
2. Ask for light to see your sins
3. Review your day - where did sin enter?
4. Ask forgiveness for sins committed
5. Resolve to grow in virtue tomorrow
"Create in me a clean heart, O God" - Psalm 51:10
