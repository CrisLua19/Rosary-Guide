🙏 Christian Life

"Walk worthy of the calling to which you have been called" - Ephesians 4:1

7 Pillars of Daily Christian Life

📖

Daily Scripture

Read God's Word every day. Start with Gospels.

"Your word is a lamp to my feet" - Psalm 119:105
🙏

Constant Prayer

Pray without ceasing. Morning, noon, night.

"Pray without ceasing" - 1 Thessalonians 5:17
🍞

Holy Eucharist

Receive Jesus frequently. Sunday Mass minimum.

"Unless you eat my flesh... no life in you" - John 6:53
❌

Confession

Monthly sacrament. Cleanse your soul regularly.

"Whose sins you forgive are forgiven" - John 20:23
❤️

Love Neighbor

Works of mercy. Help the poor, forgive enemies.

"Love your neighbor as yourself" - Mark 12:31
⛪

Sunday Worship

Never miss Holy Mass. Day of the Lord.

"Do not forsake assembling together" - Hebrews 10:25
🌹

Mary & Saints

Imitate Mary. Ask saints' intercession.

"All generations will call me blessed" - Luke 1:48

Morning Offering

O Jesus, through the Immaculate Heart of Mary,
I offer You my prayers, works, joys, and sufferings of this day
for all the intentions of Your Sacred Heart,
in union with the Holy Sacrifice of the Mass
throughout the world,
in reparation for my sins,
for the intentions of all my relatives and friends,
and in particular for the intentions of the Holy Father. Amen.

The Golden Rule

✅

DO THESE

  • Love God above all
  • Honor your parents
  • Help the poor
  • Forgive enemies
  • Speak truth
  • Be pure
❌

AVOID THESE

  • Occasions of sin
  • Bad company
  • Worldly pleasures
  • Gossip
  • Anger
  • Laziness
